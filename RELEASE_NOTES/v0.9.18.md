**The token text says what a leak is** ([Sign-in Guidance](../SPEC.md#sign-in-guidance)). The manifest and device texts are 0.9.17's, unchanged.

## What was measured

With 0.9.17's words a plain assistant signed the person in, saved the token answer to a file, read the file back to inspect it, and saw the credential in its own output. Following "a credential that appears there is leaked and must be replaced", it opened a new registration four seconds after the key was issued and asked the person to approve again — and again after that, without end.

## What changes

The token text now says:

- receiving the answer is not a leak, and neither is reading it back from where it was saved: save it as it arrived and work from there;
- a credential is leaked only when it has been shown to the person or written somewhere else — then the agent says so and asks the person to revoke it on the service and sign in again;
- the agent never starts another sign-in on its own for a credential it already holds.

## Every version's words stay valid

`discovery.json` publishes the 0.9.18 texts beside the earlier ones (`$defs/signInGuidance/$defs/0.9.18`). A service carries the texts of the version it declares, or of a later one; a service that declares 0.9.18 carries 0.9.18's words.
