The sign-in guidance names the credential: **keep it under the name the person gave the service** (its host if they gave none), so that signing in to several services in one conversation keeps each apart.

## What changes

- **The three texts** ([Sign-in Guidance](../SPEC.md#sign-in-guidance)) add the name: save the credential, and keep it, under the name the person gave this service. Another name is another sign-in, kept apart.
- **The credential block's `mcp` entry** is named after the address of the manifest the person signed in at (the one that declares `deviceAuthorizationUrl`, not the tenant manifest): its host, and its path when that manifest is not at the root. It was a service-chosen name (`remundo`, `dotquant`), so two tenants of one platform overwrote each other. The consumer keeps it under the person's name, which is that host whenever they gave none.
- **Name Resolution**: a credential bound to a name is kept under it too, and a configuration entry made from it takes the same name.

## Every version's words stay valid

`discovery.json` publishes the texts once per version that changed them (`$defs/signInGuidance/$defs/0.9.14`, `.../0.9.15`), and `authentication.note` accepts any of them. A service carries the texts of the version it declares, or of a later one. A 0.9.14 service is never failed for not having moved yet, and a service that declares 0.9.15 carries 0.9.15's words.

## Reference implementations

- `@behavioralstate/best-validate` 0.4.1 checks the words against the version a manifest declares: a 0.9.14 manifest with 0.9.14's words passes, and a 0.9.15 one with 0.9.14's words fails and is told which to carry.
