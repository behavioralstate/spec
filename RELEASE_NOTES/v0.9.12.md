Clarification release — **three rules that implementers were already guessing at**, stated. No manifest field changes; every 0.9.11 manifest remains valid. The two new requirements are stated now and **required from 0.10.0**, like 0.9.11's. `best-validate` reports a bare error from the two OAuth endpoints as a warning until then; the site sentence lives on a page no validator reads.

## What a site shows the person

*Origin Discovery* now says what a site's own "connect your assistant" page may give the person: `Sign me in to <name>` — or `Sign me up on <name>` where the platform lets people sign up — with nothing appended: no URL, no tenant ID, no steps. The name is the whole instruction; anything added is a second agent-directed description. A name that does not resolve yet is fixed with the bridge (serve or redirect `/.well-known/best` on the public origin), never with a longer sentence.

## Errors on the two OAuth endpoints

`deviceAuthorizationUrl` and `tokenUrl` answer errors per RFC 6749 §5.2 — a JSON body with `error` and, recommended, `error_description` — not in the BEST error format. A request that is not form-encoded is `invalid_request`, never a bare status with an empty body: the agent is often a model that corrects itself only from what the answer says. Conformance item 6 names the exception.

## One backend, two kinds of person

[Identity and Agent Registration](https://github.com/behavioralstate/spec/blob/main/specs/identity-and-registration.md) gains a worked example: a customer platform and the operator's back office on one BEST API are two names with two manifests. Each host serves its own root manifest and its own registration, the door is stamped on the registration so one approval page refuses the other's codes, and the consumer keeps the two credentials apart by the name each was issued under.
