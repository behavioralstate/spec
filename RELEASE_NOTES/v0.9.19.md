**Text that only one implementation needed is taken back out** ([#37](https://github.com/behavioralstate/spec/issues/37)). 0.9.18 wrote thirty-eight entries of #37 into the spec on the bar that a sentence could be written. The bar is now stricter ([SDLC.md](../SDLC.md) section 2): the text changes when two independent implementations read a sentence differently and interop fails, or when a client cannot be written from the text alone; a mechanism one implementation needs is that implementation's own, documented in its README, until a second implementation needs it. Nine entries' additions describe how best-peer runs a machine and nothing else runs that way, so this release removes them, listed below. No manifest changes and no schema changes. Nothing a 0.9.17 or 0.9.18 service does becomes wrong: the removed obligations were dated 0.10.0 and none was yet required, and the removed sentences with no date were mays and shoulds; a service that built to any of them keeps behaving as it does, and the spec just no longer says so.

## Removed

- **Commands That Wait**, the section: a command accepted before there is capacity, the wait told as an event and bounded. A service that queues is free to, as before; the spec no longer says how.
- **Forgetting Content**, the section: a holder forgetting the content of facts it sent or was told.
- **`writeOnly` fields** taken at admission and never recorded.
- **`completes`** on a command's schema document, and `progress_pct`. A consumer waits for an event in `produces`, as before.
- **`until` and `reason`** in a step 4 refusal for a state that will pass by itself.
- **A reading that changes by itself may be a query rather than an event.** Events remain immutable facts; what a service serves through a query was never constrained.
- **The work of a credential taken back** ending or going on with it. What a credential's end does to its open streams, and the bound an approver sets, stay (0.9.18).
- **Membership by the service's own commands**, and an identity named across accounts by an opaque id.

Each is documented in best-peer's README, and its entries on #37 (18, 21, 24, 42, 46, 49, 50, 51 and 52) are closed as the implementation's own.

## Kept from 0.9.18

Everything else: the clarifications two implementations read differently (idempotency's owner and the duplicate's answer, an unknown `Last-Event-ID`, `from` and `to` in RFC 3339, `token_type`, a credential with no `tenant_id` and the root listing its tenants, `415`, a correlation id bound to its first sender, `GET /events` as the log alone, a `201` processed across a restart, a workflows capability `active` only with a recipe, a query's `parameters` schema), the correlation id on every later fact about what a command created (best-mcp's `send_command_and_wait` follows it), a root that lists a credential's tenants through its catalogue (best-mcp 2.6.0 reaches each tenant by it), and the security obligations in [security.md](../specs/security.md).

## Required from 0.10.0

Conformance item 13 now lists only what stays. *Required from 0.10.0* dates when an obligation binds; it plans no release.
