**The manifest's caching headers are a must.** Since 0.9.20 a server should have sent an `ETag` and a `Cache-Control` other than `no-store` on the manifest. A should was not enough: on 26 November, of five live services, three sent both and two sent `no-store`, so a consumer of those two read the whole manifest every time. This release makes the two headers a must, and says how long a consumer may go on using the copy it holds.

A service is held to the version its manifest declares. One that declares 0.9.23 or earlier stays compliant as it is; one that declares 0.9.24 sends both headers.

## Changed

- **An `ETag` and a `Cache-Control` other than `no-store` on the manifest are a must** ([#367](https://github.com/behavioralstate/spec/issues/367)). [SPEC.md](../SPEC.md) (Discovery) says so, and Conformance lists it as item 14, from 0.9.24. A `304` to a conditional request stays conformant.

## Added

- **How long a held copy may be used.** `max-age` is how long a consumer may use its copy without asking again, not how long the service exists. A server may also state `stale-if-error` ([RFC 5861](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5861)): how long the copy may still be used when the manifest cannot be read again. A consumer that keeps a copy should use it no longer than the two allow, and not at all past `max-age` where the server stated no `stale-if-error`. This is a may for the server and a should for the consumer.

## best-validate

From its next release, 0.4.7, it reports a manifest that declares 0.9.24 or later and is sent without the two headers as a failure. Below 0.9.24 the same finding stays a warning.

## Where this came from

The discussion of REQ-PROP-5 and a proposed REQ-PROP-6 for `draft-king-dawn-requirements-02` on the dawn list on 26 November 2026, where the difference between `max-age` and `stale-if-error` was pointed out.

## The Internet-Draft

`draft-dinuzzo-best-protocol-03`, posted on 2026-11-02, is a snapshot of 0.9.20. The next revision follows this release.
