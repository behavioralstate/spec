**One version in the manifest.** A manifest carried the release version three times: at its root, on every service and on every capability. Only the root's is read. A service's and a capability's `version` are now optional, so a server can leave them out and a manifest that still sends them stays valid. `best.version` is unchanged: it is the version of this specification the manifest follows, and the one version a consumer reads.

## Changed

- **A service's and a capability's `version` are optional** ([#327](https://github.com/behavioralstate/spec/issues/327)). best-mcp and best-validate read only `best.version`, and nothing said what a service's version meant beyond semver, while `specs/versioning.md` had every service and capability repeat the release version. `discovery.json` no longer requires either field. [SPEC.md](../SPEC.md) (Services and Transport Bindings, Capability Entries, Versioning), `specs/versioning.md`, `specs/overview.md` and the manifest examples say so. The `version` of a catalogue entry, part of a schema's address in `/commands/{schema}/{version}`, is unchanged.

## The website

The Playground lists each service with its own capabilities, and says whether a service is public, opened by the sign-in or opened by a credential of its own. It signs in by the device flow (RFC 8628): the person approves in their own browser, and the credential stays in the page. It sends a credential only to the manifest's own host, and never to a public service.

## best-validate 0.4.6

It validates manifests against this release's `discovery.json`, so it passes a manifest whose services and capabilities carry no version of their own. 0.4.5, published after 0.9.21, checks the obligations of Conformance item 13 it can see from outside, as warnings, and lists what it does not check.

## The Internet-Draft

`draft-dinuzzo-best-protocol-03`, posted on 2026-11-02, is a snapshot of 0.9.20. It still lists a service's `version` as required; the next revision follows this release.
