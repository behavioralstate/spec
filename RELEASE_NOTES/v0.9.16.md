**The tenants root says where a credential belongs** (Multi-Tenancy rule 6). No manifest changes, and no change to the sign-in guidance.

## What was measured

A general-purpose assistant signed a person in to a multi-tenant service. The token answer carried the credential, its `tenant_id` and its `endpoint`, but the assistant kept only the credential. It then called the tenants root, the root manifest's tenant-scoped `http.endpoint`, and received an empty `404`. It told the person they had no account.

## The rule

A `GET` on the tenants root **should** answer:

- with a credential: `200` and the tenants it opens, `{ "tenants": [ { "tenant_id", "endpoint", "manifest" } ] }`;
- without one: the `401` a tenant endpoint gives, naming the way in;
- where the service cannot tell which tenant a credential opens: a BEST error saying where the consumer finds it (the token answer's `endpoint` and `tenant_id`, or a new sign-in).

Never a bare `404`. A consumer that kept only its credential finds its way back from there.
